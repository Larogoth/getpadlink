Send feedback

Something confusing, something that didn't work, or an idea? Tell us. There are no wrong answers: if you had to hunt for something, that's exactly what we want to hear.

Open the feedback form

It's a short Google Form: what you were trying to do, what happened, and a screenshot if you have one. One thing per form is best, and you can send as many as you like.

The feedback form is almost ready. For now, tell us on PadLink's contact page, or tell the person who asked you to try PadLink.

What helps the most

  • What you were trying to do, in your own words: "put a worksheet on the board", "let my phone join as a student".
  • Where you got stuck or what surprised you. Even small things, like a button you couldn't find.
  • A screenshot if you can. Windows: Windows key + Shift + S. iPad or iPhone: the top (or side) button + volume up.

Trying PadLink for the first time? The Testing PadLink page walks you through it.

The form is a Google Form: what you type and any screenshot go to PadLink's developer and are only used to make PadLink better. See the privacy policy.
